# Post-Launch Story Design Backlog

Status: `POST-LAUNCH BACKLOG`

These Story gallery items are intentionally preserved for a follow-up polish branch and are not part of the initial production launch unless they cause actual horizontal scrolling, broken content, or unusable interaction.

1. Unequal Story gallery outer margins.
2. Right-edge gallery overflow or modules touching the viewport boundary.
3. Grid children escaping the centred shell.
4. Weak non-couple photographs in prominent positions.
5. Partial subjects and awkward crops.
6. Couple-first hierarchy inconsistencies.
7. Supporting family and guest imagery receiving excessive scale.
8. Ratio and focal-point mismatches.
9. Every module needing exact 12-column validation.
10. Desktop, tablet and mobile needing separate gallery verification.
